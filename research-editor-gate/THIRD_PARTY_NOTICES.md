# Third-party notices and design lineage

Research Editor Gate is an original implementation. No third-party source code, prompt file, or private ARIS implementation is vendored in this repository.

Its design was informed at the level of general editorial principles by:

- [CCFA-Skills](https://github.com/mikubaka88/CCFA-Skills), by Chaoyue Li, MIT License: separation of writing, review, integrity checks, and evidence-aware handoffs.
- [no-ai-slop](https://github.com/petergyang/no-ai-slop), by Peter Yang, MIT License: minimum effective editing, voice preservation, and observable-pattern review rather than AI-authorship guessing.
- Academic humanization and ARIS-style workflows maintained in Hanyue Shen's research environment: semantic preservation, terminology consistency, claim-evidence gates, and explicit return of research-dependent work. Their private instructions and implementation are not reproduced here.
